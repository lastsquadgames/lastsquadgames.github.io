Privacy Policy for Last Squad

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Last Squad ("the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is developed by LastSquad Games ("we", "us").

Summary

  • You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or any contact details.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, including your advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device: level reached, coins, stars, upgrades, weapons and skins you unlocked, daily reward and mission progress, and your sound setting. This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and funded by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android device advertising identifier)Showing and measuring ads; personalized ads where you have allowed them
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, measurement and fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and compatibility
Ad interactions (ads viewed, tapped or watched)Rewards, measurement and fraud prevention
Crash and diagnostic data from the ads SDKKeeping ads working reliably

This information is collected and processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We only see aggregated, anonymous reports (for example, total ad impressions and earnings), never information that identifies you.

Rewarded ads

Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch a video in exchange for an in-game reward such as coins, a revive or extra soldiers. Watching them is never required to play.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button on the game's main menu.
  • Personalized ads: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.
  • Deleting data: uninstall the app or clear its storage to delete all game data on your device. For data held by Google, see Google's privacy tools at myaccount.google.com.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's own policies. Network traffic between the app and Google's ad servers is encrypted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google using the links above; you can also contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
